RailEase wallet update

Changes:
- Older confirmed bookings with payment_status Paid can now be cancelled.
- Cancellation credits 60% of original booking fare to the user's wallet.
- Booking form includes an option to use wallet balance first.
- Wallet amount is debited when a temporary booking hold is created.
- Remaining amount is shown in the phone QR demo payment.
- If QR payment fails or the 2-minute hold expires, wallet funds used for that booking are restored.
- If wallet covers the full fare, booking confirms without a QR payment.
- Wallet debits/credits are recorded in wallet_transactions.

Existing SQLite databases are migrated automatically when the backend starts.
This is an in-app demo wallet; no actual bank or UPI money is moved.
